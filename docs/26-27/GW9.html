The Week in Words
GW9 — an editorial nobody asked for
The Disgraces
Martin Borizanov takes the wooden spoon on 29, 24 below the league average, with Verbruggen's 11 and Calafiori's 6 both watching from his bench. Last week this column withdrew its kind word for Stephen Rudd after the Schade captaincy, and this week Stevo did what everyone asked: he gave the armband to Haaland, along with 20 other managers. City lost 1-0 at home to Brighton and Haaland scored 2. Schade, sitting in Stevo's own midfield with no armband, scored 3. That makes five blank armbands for Releven Tigers FC, still the most in the league, and it is hard to know what advice is left. Rahul Vahi went further and played his Triple Captain on Haaland, which got him 6 points from the armband and 2 from the chip. Vignesh Ramesh paid 4 points to sell Palmer and Barry, who scored 8 and 9, for Tavernier and Isak, who scored 3 and 2, the week's worst hit at −16 net. Claude-Noel Tamakloe would have scored 69 without touching his team and scored 47 instead. Mij left 32 points on the bench, the most in the league, while Jay Curtis left 26 there, scored 40 and fell from second to fourth.
The Shockers
Nobody saw this coming, and the table says it still doesn't matter: Kris McCarthy scored 74, the week's top score by 2 and his best of the season. He was one of only two managers who didn't captain Haaland, and N.Jackson repaid him with 18. He did it in spite of Newcastle, who lost 1-0 at home to Everton, a second straight home league defeat by that score. Barnes managed 2 for him, and St James' Park is still waiting for its first clean sheet of the season. Sporting Club MarRow remain bottom on 498, now 3 behind North London Forever. The other armband rebel was Alexis Dritsas, whose Palmer captaincy returned 16 in a week of 71. Nnaemeka Ezugwu made 72 before a 4-point hit. Rahul did redeem himself with his transfers: four moves took him from a stand-pat 33 to 57, led by Shaw to Konsa for 10 net, the transfer of the week. And Aman Sharma got a late present when FPL finally applied Verbruggen's autosub on Tuesday, adding 11 and lifting him from tenth to seventh.
The Title Race
Bluehomie (YouTube) scored 67 and turned a 2-point lead into 23, on 631. That is a fifth week on top, and he did it with the same 4-point armband as almost everyone else, because Konsa, Rogers, De Cuyper, N.Jackson and Barry all delivered around it. Amit Putty climbed 4 places to second on 608, the biggest rise of the week. Anti Algo FPL played the Free Hit, fielded Van Hecke for 15, and beat the template XI by exactly 1 point, with the same captain as 20 other managers. The man who isn't algo was, for one week at least, the algo plus one. By the report's reckoning the chip cost him 9 against his regular team. Pamparam sit third on 607, 1 point behind him, with It was actually Eze on 602, Rice to meet you on 600 and Son of a Doge on 596. Last week the top six were covered by 18 points. It is now 35, with 29 gameweeks remaining.
