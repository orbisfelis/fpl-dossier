GW8 — an editorial nobody asked for
The Disgraces
Darius Goodarzi and Amit Putty share the wooden spoon on 33, 13 below the league average, and they got there the same way: by giving the armband to João Pedro, who picked up an injury in training and never got on the pitch. Darius's vice-captain was Palmer, who was sent off against Spurs, so his armband came out at −2. Last week this column offered Stephen Rudd a kind word. It has now been withdrawn. With 14 managers handing the armband to Haaland for 16, Stevo gave his to Schade, away at Hull. Schade managed 2 in a 1-1 draw, and Releven Tigers FC now have four blank armbands this season, the most in the league, on their own. Spurs, meanwhile, won 2-0 at Stamford Bridge for a second league win in a row, and Stevo was involved after all: Marmoush came off his bench for João Pedro and scored 2. The man who cannot pick a captain could not even pick his own Spurs player. At the bottom, Kris McCarthy posted 42 and stays last on 424, 6 behind North London Forever. Newcastle won 2-0 at Crystal Palace and Barnes scored 8 for him, which counts as Kris's best week as a Newcastle owner. Newcastle have now kept two clean sheets this season, both away from home. St James' Park is still waiting for one.
The Shockers
21 of the league's 23 managers started João Pedro, and the week belonged to one of the two who didn't. Jay Curtis scored 69, the week's top score by 14, and climbed 5 places to second. Kelvin Ampene made the transfer of the week, swapping Gvardiol for Gabriel just in time for a goal, a clean sheet and 17 points, 16 net. Claude-Noel Tamakloe played the week's Bench Boost and got 5 points from it, then captained Palmer for −2, which is about as much as a chip week can go wrong without taking a hit. Sahil Vora left 20 points on his bench, the most in the league. Pamparam's Wildcard came out 19 points better than the old team would have managed, mostly thanks to Konsa, who scored 14 from a goal and a clean sheet.
The Title Race
Bluehomie (YouTube) is back on top, on 564, 2 clear of It was actually Eze. That is his fourth week on top, more than anyone else this season. He also captained João Pedro, but his vice-captain was Haaland, so the armband still returned 16. Last week's leader had no such luck. Amit Putty will tell anyone who listens that he isn't 'algo', and this week the one choice that set him apart from the template, João Pedro over Haaland, dropped him from first to sixth, 18 back. His armband passed to Rogers for 2. Pamparam are third on 551, 13 back, ahead of Son of a Doge on 549 and Calafiori Dreamin' on 547. The top six are covered by 18 points with 30 gameweeks remaining.
The Nerd Corner
