The Season in Words
Pre-season 2026/2027 — an editorial nobody asked for
Unfinished Business
RLN ⭐️ won it by 64 points, which reads comfortable and absolutely was not — the team that led this league for eighteen weeks was Son of a Doge, and he finished fourth. Pamparam ⭐️⭐️⭐️ took second on 2309, close enough to taste it and far enough to sit through the trophy photo. That is the business this league did not finish, and it carries over whole. Three of last season's twenty-one are not coming back: Darius Goodarzi, whose 2238 was eighth, Aarif Kassam on 2141, and Chris B, who signed up for this one and thought better of it before a ball was kicked. Everyone else returns, including two who left it late enough that the group chat had already written them off — Jay Curtis and Martin Borizanov both joined in the last few days. Curtis is worth a second look before you dismiss the man who came seventeenth: his 2647 in 2021/22 ranked 5,677 in the world, and if that number looks familiar it is because Pamparam scored exactly the same total that season and finished 5,513. Two of them, same points, one hundred and sixty-four places apart, five years ago. The genuinely new faces are Evros Stephanou (eight seasons, best of 2420), Vignesh Ramesh (three, best of 2326) and Panagiotis Pyrgas, one season deep and nothing to lose. So it is 21 of you this time, a new league ID, a fresh set of entry numbers, and every single one of you back to zero. The deadline is tomorrow and the drafts are not finished, whatever anyone tells the group chat.
The Market
The game has decided that Erling Haaland is worth £15.5m and 73.3% of the world has agreed without argument, which makes him less a pick than a tax. Bruno Fernandes at £12.0m is the only other player north of ten, and behind them the market has quietly reorganised itself around defenders: every serious defensive-contribution scorer now starts at £5.0m or more, because the pricing team finally read the same spreadsheets you did. João Pedro has been bid up to 57.2% ownership on the back of eight pre-season goals, and Szoboszlai sits at 41.9% on the promise of a floor rather than a ceiling. The value has drained out of the middle and collected at the bottom, where Truffert at £5.5m and Tyrick Mitchell at £4.5m both returned 30 points per million of this season's price. Fixtures point the same way early: Liverpool have the kindest opening five at an average difficulty of 2.6, with Leeds, Man City, Man United and Spurs all level behind on 2.8. Build the spine cheap, spend what is left on the two names everyone owns anyway, and accept that August rewards the boring.
Ones to Watch
Marcos Senesi at £6.0m is the most underpriced asset in the game and it is not especially close — 52 defensive-contribution points last season at a 70% hit rate, and only 9.8% of managers have noticed. Elliot Anderson matched him on 52 from midfield at a 68% clip for £6.5m, which is a midfielder with a defender's floor and none of the anxiety. James Tarkowski (£6.0m, 44 points, 59%) is the third name in that tier and the cheapest way to stop thinking about your back line until October. For the ones the market has mispriced downward, Yeremy at £5.5m underperformed his expected numbers by 6.7 — four returns from 10.7 expected, which is not decline, it is a coin that has landed badly eleven times. Mateta tells the same story more expensively at £6.5m, 3.7 short of his underlying figures. And if you want the fixture angle, Marc Guéhi at £6.0m and Van Hecke at £5.0m both play for sides in that 2.8 opening bracket, which is a lot of cheap defence pointed at a soft August.
The Warnings
Antoine Semenyo scored 17 goals from 11.1 expected last season, beat his xG by 5.9, and 28.4% of the game has decided that was skill rather than a hot streak — at £8.5m he is the single most likely disappointment in your squad. Gibbs-White carries the same problem in a cheaper shirt. The template is its own trap: owning Haaland means you tread water with three-quarters of the field, and fading him means you either win this league or explain yourself in April, with no third outcome available. As for the people across the table, the numbers are not reassuring. Pamparam ⭐️⭐️⭐️ has a career best of 2647 and sixteen seasons of evidence, 2UCL > Arsenal ☆☆ has 2552 across eighteen, and both RLN ⭐️ and Son of a Doge have cleared 2516 — all four have finished inside the top 1% of the world at least once. Your genuine unknown is Evros Stephanou, who nobody here has played against and who has already gone 2420 in a season. Four days. Pick a captain and stop opening the app.
